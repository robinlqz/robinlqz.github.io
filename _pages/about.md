I graduated with a PhD in Economics from the University of Nevada, Reno. I also hold an MSc in Economics, Finance, and Management from the University of Bristol and a BBA in Economics from the Chinese University of Hong Kong. My research focuses on development economics and behavioral economics. My work has been published in reputable journals, including the Australian Economic Papers, Asian Development Review, Tourism and Hospitality, and Chinese Studies.

我拥有内华达大学雷诺分校（University of Nevada, Reno）的经济学博士学位，此前还获得了布里斯托大学（University of Bristol）的经济、金融与管理理学硕士学位，以及香港中文大学（Chinese University of Hong Kong）的经济学工商管理学士学位。我的研究领域主要集中在发展经济学和行为经济学。我的研究成果已在《澳大利亚经济论文》（Australian Economic Papers）、《亚洲发展评论》（Asian Development Review）、《旅游与酒店管理》（Tourism and Hospitality）及《中国研究》（Chinese Studies）等知名学术期刊上发表。
